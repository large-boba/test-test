## Executive Summary

This report analyses the current catalogues from seven major Australian pharmacy and retail chains. The dominant themes this fortnight are **winter wellness**, **fragrance & gifting**, and **vitamin sale events**. All pharmacy chains are heavily promoting loyalty apps and digital engagement, while supermarket chains are focusing on half-price weekly specials with integrated loyalty reward multipliers.

## Key Industry Observations

1. **Winter Wellness Dominance:** All pharmacy catalogues are pushing cold & flu, vitamin C, and immune-support products reflecting June seasonality in Australia.

2. **Loyalty App Competition Intensifying:** CW, Amcal, TerryWhite Chemmart, Woolworths, and HealthSave all actively promoting digital loyalty engagement. TerryWhite's myTWC app (script ordering + rewards) and Woolworths' Everyday Rewards + Healthylife ecosystem are the most integrated.

3. **Fragrance as Hero:** Chemist Warehouse is leaning hard into perfume/fragrance as a gifting-season driver heading into mid-year (pre-school holidays), setting it apart from pure health-and-wellness messaging.

4. **Supermarkets Encroaching on Pharmacy:** Woolworths' **10x Everyday Rewards on Health & Beauty** and dedicated vitamins half-price page, combined with Healthylife.com.au, represents a strong competitive move into the pharmacy category.

5. **Medibank x Amcal Partnership:** Noteworthy B2B2C promotional vehicle – linking health insurance loyalty to pharmacy purchases is a differentiated proposition not seen in competitors this cycle.

6. **Price Aggression:** Direct Chemist Outlet's blanket ½ Price Vitamin Sale positions it as the most aggressive on price in the pharmacy sector this period.
